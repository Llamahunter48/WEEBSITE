Summit Seekers

Summit Seekers is a South Island–based guiding service run by local outdoor enthusiasts who love sharing New Zealand’s mountain trails. They focus on beginner-friendly hikes, simple and honest gear advice, and safe guided trips, all with small groups and clear communication so people new to hiking can build confidence and enjoy the mountains.

Aoraki/Mount Cook

Aoraki/Mount Cook is New Zealand’s highest mountain in the Southern Alps. Most people don’t climb the peak; they hike valley tracks to view it. The main walk is the Hooker Valley Track, which is easy and takes about 3 hours return to reach a lake with a clear view of Aoraki.

Roys Peak Track (Wanaka)

Roys Peak Track is a steep, well‑formed summit hike just outside Wānaka, climbing through farmland and tussock to a famous viewpoint high above Lake Wānaka with panoramic views of the Southern Alps. The track is about 16 km return with a big steady uphill the whole way, and most people take around 5–6 hours to complete the return hike, including time for photos at the lookout and near the summit.

Mount Taranaki

Mount Taranaki (Taranaki Maunga) is a 2,518 m cone-shaped volcano in Egmont National Park, known for steep slopes, fast-changing weather and big coastal views. The standard North Egmont summit route is roughly 12–13 km return and usually takes about 8–9 hours total for a fit, well-prepared person in good summer conditions (around 5–6 hours up and 3–4 hours down).
